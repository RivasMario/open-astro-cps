Side 1 (Purple) Backlight Illuminator
Controls the high-intensity green/white LED backlight behind the LCD screen and keypad buttons.
How to use: Press once in dark or night conditions. Illuminates for 5 seconds on a countdown timer so you can read channel names without killing battery. Pressing any other key resets the 5-second timer.
Side 2 (1-Dot) Zone Scan Toggle (Z Icon)
Turns the automated channel scanning receiver on and off for the active zone.
Why you need it: Team members do not always stay on one channel (some speak on TEAM CLEAR, others on TEAM ENCRYPT, others on repeaters). Pressing 1-Dot activates the Z icon. The radio listens to all zone channels automatically and locks onto any incoming transmission instantly.
Side 3 (2-Dot) Monitor / Squelch Bypass
Bypasses the digital/analog squelch filter to listen to open channel noise or faint/distant team units.
How to use: Press and hold when you suspect someone is trying to transmit from fringe range or out of repeater coverage. Emits open receiver hiss and bypasses NAC/PL filters so you can hear faint carriers.
LCD Display Screen Icon Decoder
Decodes what the symbols on the top status line actually mean:
• Ø (Circle with Slash): ADP Encryption Active. Displayed on Channel 2 (TEAM ENCRYPT), 5, 10, 12, 14, 16. Confirms that Software Key 1 (A1B2C3D4E5) is active and your voice is scrambled against scanners and eavesdroppers.
• Z: Scan Active. The radio is actively sweeping channels in the zone.
• H / L: High Power (3W) or Low Power (1W) transmitter mode.
• ||||: Received signal strength indicator (4 bars = maximum local repeater reception).
Top Orange Tx Power Toggle (High / Low)
Switches RF transmitter output power between High (3 Watts) and Low (1 Watt).
How to use: Press to toggle. When working up close on simplex tactical channels, set to L (Low Power) to reduce battery drain and prevent signal overload. When operating over long range or through heavy buildings, set to H (High Power).
Toggle Switch (A/B/C) Rapid Zone Selector
Direct physical 3-position lever for instant zone selection without using menus:
• Position A: Zone 1 (TEAM COMM) → Primary team tactical & ADP channels.
• Position B: Zone 2 (800 INTEROP) → Regional emergency mutual aid channels.
• Position C: Zone 3 (TAC OPS) → Secondary operational channels.
